If it is not on your check

  • Ask your manager in writing, by text is fine. Keep their answer.1
  • You can contact the city's Department of Consumer and Worker Protection: call 311 and ask for Fair Workweek.2
  • Asking about your rights is protected. Your employer is not allowed to punish you for it.3
